Selected work: SIPEKA driver drowsiness detection

A real-time prototype developed at the Embedded and Network System Laboratory and used as my final project. It placed 3rd in the Innovation Track at the Huawei ICT Competition 2024–2025 National Final and is listed as a patent (April 2026).

1
Person & ROI detection

YOLOv8n (conf > 0.5) isolates the driver bounding box as a grayscale ROI.

2
68-Landmark analysis

Dlib extracts eye contours, mouth opening, and eye-center tilt angle inside the ROI.

3
Multi-modal fatigue decision

Triggers when EAR < 0.25 (3s), MAR > 0.15 (5s), or |Tilt| > 15° (60s).

4
Audio alarm & REST API

Plays music.wav via Pygame and dispatches async JSON telemetry to the server.
